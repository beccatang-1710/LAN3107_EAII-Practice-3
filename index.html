EAII Practice 3 — Meetings: Jolly Juicy Business Expansion

Presented by Rebecca Tang

Task Overview

You are on the marketing team of Jolly Juicy, a beverage shop in Hong Kong. You and your colleagues are going to hold a meeting to discuss plans to expand the business. You should:

  • give your own suggestion(s) on the agenda items
  • explain the reason(s) for your suggestion(s)
  • listen actively and respond to others' suggestions by expressing preferences, agreement, or disagreement
  • reach a consensus on agenda items 2–4

As a group, you must cover all the agenda items below.

Meeting Details

Jolly Juicy Marketing Team 16 December 20-- at 2:30 pm in the Meeting Room

Agenda

  1. Open the meeting
  2. New beverages on the drink list
  3. Tactics for promoting the new offer

    Possible tactics:

    • Spokesperson
    • Online platforms
    • Promotional booths
    • Free trial
  4. AOB

    Ways to attract new customers:

    • Referral scheme
    • Cash vouchers
  5. Close the meeting

Language Features

Green tags in the conversation show explanations of the use of language (modal verbs + bare infinitive, cohesive devices, contractions, suggestion patterns, etc.). A ✗ marks the incorrect form (e.g. can + bought), which should be the bare infinitive (e.g. can buy).

Language functions in bold print

Communicative functions (e.g. making suggestions, showing agreement, giving reasons) are highlighted in the italic blue line above each speaker's turn.

Cohesive devices / Linking words

  • Besides / Moreover / Furthermore (dated)
  • In addition / Additionally
  • Therefore / Thus / Hence / So

Contractions

I'm (I am) / It'll (It will) / We've (We have) / Let's (Let us) / That's (That is)

Thinking words (air fillers)

Well… Ummm… uhhhh…

Modal verbs

  • Ability: can, could   Possibility: may, might
  • Permission: Can, May   Obligation: must, have to
  • Prohibition: Can't, Mustn't   Offers: Will, Would
  • Suggestions: Shall, Should   Requests: Could, Would
  • Future Possibility: Will, Shall   Past Probability: Could have, Would have

+ action verb (bare infinitive) + object (noun clause), e.g. Customers can buy our new drinks at a cheaper price.

Making suggestions (action verbs: suggest + advise + recommend)

I suggest / advise / recommend to watch a K-pop concert.  ✗

I suggest / advise / recommend watching a K-pop concert.  ✓

I suggest / advise / recommend that you (should — British style) watch a K-pop concert.  ✓

How about buy instant noodles?  ✗    How about buying instant noodles?  ✓

Sentence structures

  • Cause & effect: Since / Because / As + S + V + O, S + V + O.
  • But / However + S + V + O.
